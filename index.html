Clear, direct answers to help you plan your itinerary, choose the right vehicle, and budget accurately.

How much does a taxi from Shimla to Kaza cost?

A private commercial taxi from Shimla to Kaza typically costs between INR [PRICE] and INR [PRICE] depending on whether you choose an Ertiga, Innova Crysta, or Tempo Traveller.

Because the journey traverses rugged Kinnaur cliff roads and spans at least two days with an overnight halt in Kalpa or Tabo, one-way rates factor in return empty running charges mandated by local taxi union guidelines. The price includes vehicle hire, fuel, hill commercial taxes, toll plazas, and the driver's daily food and lodging allowance. Night halt charges are set at INR [NIGHT HALT CHARGE] per night. To ensure full clarity before you board your flight from Bengaluru or train from Delhi, we issue an itemized written quote with every component spelled out in advance. Check our dedicated Shimla to Kaza taxi fares for detailed vehicle comparisons.

Which vehicle is best for Spiti Valley roads?

The Toyota Innova Crysta is the most reliable and comfortable vehicle for Spiti Valley roads due to its robust chassis, 178 mm ground clearance, and proven high-altitude reliability.

While hatchbacks and standard low-clearance sedans can reach Reckong Peo and Sangla in Kinnaur, they frequently scrape bottom or get stranded on the water crossings (mallings) and rocky riverbeds between Kaza, Losar, Kunzum Pass, and Batal. For budget-conscious families of up to 4 passengers, the Maruti Ertiga performs well across paved sections, but larger families or groups with heavy luggage require an Innova Crysta or a 4x4 / Tempo Traveller. Explore full specifications on our Innova Crysta vehicle guide before finalizing your itinerary.

When is the Spiti road via Kunzum Pass open?

The Kunzum Pass road connecting Kaza to Manali is officially open only during the summer and autumn months, typically from late June until mid-October [MONTHS].

For the remaining seven to eight months of the year, Kunzum Pass (14,931 ft) remains buried under deep snow drifts, cutting off direct vehicular transit to Manali and Rohtang Tunnel. However, the southern route into Spiti Valley via Shimla, Rampur, Reckong Peo, and Kaza remains open almost year-round, weather and snow-clearance permitting. If you plan to complete the full circular loop including Chandratal Lake, you must schedule your dates between July and September. Review our seasonal advisory on the Spiti–Kinnaur circuit itinerary.

How many days does the Spiti–Kinnaur circuit take?

A safe, enjoyable Spiti–Kinnaur circuit tour requires a minimum of 9 to 10 days starting from Shimla and concluding in Manali or Chandigarh.

Attempting this 1,200 km Himalayan loop in fewer days introduces severe altitude sickness risks, as the road climbs from 2,200 meters in Shimla to over 4,500 meters at Kunzum Pass. A standard recommended itinerary includes night halts at Sarahan, Sangla (or Chitkul), Kalpa, Tabo, two to three nights in Kaza for high-altitude villages like Hikkim and Komik, one night near Chandratal Lake, and a final descent to Manali. Spreading the drive ensures you travel only 4 to 6 hours each day, leaving ample daylight for monasteries, photography, and gentle acclimatization. Read our complete day-by-day Spiti tour itinerary.
