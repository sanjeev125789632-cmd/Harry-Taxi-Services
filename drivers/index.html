Safety Protocol

Our Mountain Road Driving Standards

Strict operational protocols followed by every driver operating under the Harry Taxi Services banner.

Zero Night Driving in Remote High Passes

We do not drive between Losar, Kunzum Pass, Chandratal, and Batal after dark. Falling temperatures at night cause water streams to freeze into dangerous black ice, while visibility drops drastically. All driving on high-altitude segments concludes by 5:30 PM.

Altitude Sickness Awareness & First Aid

Our drivers are trained to recognize the early onset of Acute Mountain Sickness (AMS)—headache, loss of appetite, breathlessness. If a passenger shows symptoms, the driver carries portable oxygen and knows the exact descent elevation profiles to ensure quick recovery.

Uphill Right of Way & Courtesy

Our drivers strictly adhere to Himalayan mountain road ethics: uphill climbing vehicles always have priority at single-lane passing points. We never engage in aggressive overtaking on blind curves.

Daily Vehicle Inspection

Every morning before turning the ignition, our drivers check brake fluid levels, coolant, tyre pressure (including spare tyre), suspension bushes, and test the horn and fog lights.

Travel with Verified Mountain Drivers

We assign your specific verified driver and share their contact information 24 hours prior to your journey.
