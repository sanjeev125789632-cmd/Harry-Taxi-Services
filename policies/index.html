Booking, Payment & Cancellation Policies

Because mountain expeditions require dedicated vehicle preparation, fuel planning, and driver scheduling, our terms are formulated for mutual security and transparency. Every quote is delivered with these written rules.

Section 1

Advance Amount & Balance Payment Timing

1. Booking Confirmation Advance

To block a commercial vehicle and reserve a dedicated mountain driver for your dates, an advance deposit of [ADVANCE %] of the total package fare is required upon confirmation. Once this advance is received via bank transfer (NEFT/RTGS) or verified UPI, an official written booking confirmation receipt is issued via WhatsApp and email.

2. Balance Payment Timing

The remaining balance is payable according to the following schedule:

  • Single-Day Transfers (e.g. Chandigarh to Manali, Delhi to Shimla): 100% of the remaining balance is payable directly to the driver upon reaching your destination hotel.
  • Multi-Day Circuits (e.g. 10-Day Spiti–Kinnaur Loop): [BALANCE PAYMENT TIMING: e.g. 50% on Day 1 upon boarding, remainder on Day 5 or before entering the Kaza-Manali crossing].
  • Payment Modes: Bank transfer (NEFT/IMPS/UPI) or cash directly to the operator. Please note that mobile internet coverage disappears in remote Spiti zones, so electronic transfers must be completed before leaving Reckong Peo or Kaza.
Section 2

Cancellation & Refund Slabs

If you need to cancel or postpone your trip due to personal emergencies or flight changes, our cancellation charges are calculated based on the number of days prior to the journey start date:

Cancellation Notice Period Refund Percentage Cancellation Charge
More than 15 days before start date [REFUND %: e.g. 90%] Refund [10%] Processing & banking deduction
Between 7 and 14 days before start date [REFUND %: e.g. 50%] Refund [50%] of the advance deposit
Less than 7 days before start date / No Show [REFUND %: 0%] No Refund 100% of the advance deposit forfeited (vehicle committed)

* Date rescheduling requests received more than 10 days in advance are accommodated free of charge, subject to vehicle availability for the newly requested dates.

Section 3

Weather, Landslide & Roadblock Delay Policy

The Himalayas are dynamic and unpredictable. Monsoon cloudbursts, rockfalls near Akpa/Tapri in Kinnaur, or sudden snowfall at Kunzum Pass can lead to temporary road closures enforced by the Border Roads Organisation (BRO) and Himachal Police.

Safety First Principle

Our drivers have full authority to halt travel or choose alternate safe routes if road conditions pose an immediate danger to passenger life. We never attempt high-risk night driving or bypass active police barricades.

Standard Protocol in Case of Road Closures:

  • Temporary Roadblock (< 6 hours): The driver waits safely at the designated roadside clearing point. No extra charges are applied.
  • Major Roadblock Requiring Alternate Route: If a route is impassable (e.g., Kinnaur highway blocked, requiring rerouting via Mandi/Manali or vice versa), the vehicle continues via the alternate route. Fuel and toll variations for significant extra distance are billed at the agreed per-km rate [EXTRA KM RATE].
  • Force Majeure Halts (Stuck midway due to landslide): Guest hotel accommodation and food expenses at intermediate unplanned halts are borne directly by the guest. Driver boarding is covered by our company.

All extra charges must be noted on the official trip duty slip signed by the guest and driver at the conclusion of the journey.
