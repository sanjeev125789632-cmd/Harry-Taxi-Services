Frequently Asked Questions About Hiring a Tempo Traveller for Spiti

Key details for group planners and corporate organizers.

Can a Tempo Traveller go to Spiti Valley?

Yes, our modified 12-seater Force Tempo Travellers operate successfully across the entire Spiti–Kinnaur circuit, including high passes like Kunzum Pass.

Operating a minibus across the narrow cliff roads of Kinnaur and the unpaved riverbeds between Kaza and Batal requires an experienced driver who understands mountain passing points and uphill clearance. Our Tempo Travellers are outfitted with heavy-duty rear leaf springs and a high 200 mm axle clearance that easily crosses water streams (mallings) without grounding. Large panoramic windows ensure that every member of your group enjoys unimpeded views of the snow-capped Himalayan ranges throughout the journey. See full route details on our Spiti circuit guide.

How many seats are in a Tempo Traveller for Spiti Valley?

Our Spiti-specific Tempo Travellers are configured with 12 reclining passenger seats arranged in a spacious 2x1 configuration plus the driver.

While commercial city variants often squeeze 16 to 20 seats into the same chassis, high-altitude mountain travel requires generous legroom, wide aisles, and reclining pushback seats with armrests to prevent leg cramping during 5-hour driving stretches. Our 12-seater layout leaves generous interior cabin space for carry-on backpacks, heavy woolens, and camera gear. All large luggage is stowed on the waterproof roof carrier.
