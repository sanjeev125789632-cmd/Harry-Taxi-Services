Our Air Conditioning (AC) Policy in Mountain Terrains

Transparent explanation of how vehicle AC operates on steep Himalayan climbs.

1. On Plains & Expressways (Delhi, Chandigarh, Punjab, Haryana): Full air conditioning is kept running continuously at your preferred temperature.

2. In Steep Mountain Gradients: Vehicle air conditioning compressors draw substantial horsepower from the engine. On steep mountain climbs (such as ascending to Kunzum Pass, Rohtang, or Narkanda), drivers switch off the AC compressor to ensure maximum engine power, prevent engine overheating, and guarantee safe overtaking. The natural mountain air at these altitudes is crisp, cool, and clean.

3. Stationary & Traffic Stalls: AC is kept operational during traffic halts and city crossings as requested.
