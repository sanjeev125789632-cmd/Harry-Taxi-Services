Transparent Policy

What Is Always Included and Excluded in Our Taxi Fares

We eliminate ambiguity. When you receive a booking confirmation from Harry Taxi Services, the boundaries of your fare are fixed in writing.

Always Included in Your Fare

  • Exclusive dedicated commercial vehicle with yellow HP tourist permit plate
  • Fuel costs for the entire agreed itinerary distance and sightseeing points
  • All highway toll plaza payments (Fastag) along the route
  • State border passenger entry taxes (Himachal, Punjab, Haryana, Delhi)
  • Municipal parking fees at designated hotel and monument parking lots
  • Driver's daily food and lodging allowance (no extra boarding cost to guest)
  • Driver mountain navigation and snow chain handling expertise

Always Excluded (Payable Directly by Guest)

  • Guest personal hotel accommodation and meals (unless custom tour opted)
  • Monument entrance fees, monastery entry tickets, and national park fees
  • Special wildlife or wildlife sanctuary permits (e.g. Kibber wildlife zone)
  • Personal porterage, mule hire, or adventure activities (rafting, paragliding)
  • Detours beyond agreed itinerary points (billed at extra-km rate [EXTRA KM RATE])
  • Night halt charge if halting overnight on a single-day booked route [NIGHT HALT CHARGE]
  • Waiting charges beyond 45 minutes for flight/train delays [WAITING CHARGE]
