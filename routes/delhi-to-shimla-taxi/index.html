Trip Guide

Delhi to Shimla Highway Segments & Food Halts

What to expect on this scenic 7 to 8 hour road journey climbing from the plains into the pine-covered hills.

Highway Stops & Route Landmarks

  • Delhi to Murthal (50 km): Fast departure out of Delhi NCR; famous for 24x7 dhabas (Amrik Sukhdev, Gulshan).
  • Murthal to Panipat & Karnal (80 km): Elevated 6-lane NH-44 highway bypass; convenient halt at Karnal Lake Oasis.
  • Karnal to Ambala & Zirakpur (95 km): Transition from Grand Trunk Road towards the Himalayan foothills.
  • Zirakpur to Himalayan Expressway & Parwanoo (35 km): The modern elevated bypass avoiding Kalka and Pinjore traffic, leading to the entrance of Himachal Pradesh at Timber Trail.
  • Parwanoo to Solan & Shimla (85 km): Four-lane mountain highway through Dharampur, Kumarhatti, and Solan bypass, followed by scenic curves through Taradevi into Shimla (2,200 m).

Mall Road Vehicle Regulations & Drop Points

The Ridge & Mall Road Restriction: Shimla's iconic Mall Road and The Ridge are strictly pedestrian-only zones designated as Sealed/Restricted roads by the Himachal Government. No commercial taxi can drive onto Mall Road itself.

Convenient Drop Points: Our drivers drop you at the closest accessible points: the Tourism Lift on Cart Road, Victory Tunnel, Old ISBT, or directly at your hotel driveway if situated along Circular Road, Chotta Shimla, or Sanjauli.

Continuing to Spiti? Shimla serves as the ideal launchpad for the Spiti–Kinnaur circuit. We can organize a seamless transfer continuing onward to Sarahan and Sangla.

Can a Delhi to Shimla taxi drop us at hotels near Mall Road?

A taxi can take you as close as Cart Road or the Tourism Lift, but cannot drive directly onto Mall Road due to strict pedestrian-only regulations enforced by the local administration.

Mall Road and The Ridge are designated as non-vehicular zones. If your hotel is located on Mall Road, our driver parks at the designated passenger unloading bay near the Cart Road Lift. From there, you take the municipal lift directly up to the Mall Road promenade in under two minutes, with local porters (coolies) available to carry your baggage. For hotels located outside the sealed zone—such as in Chotta Shimla, Sanjauli, or Mashobra—our driver drops you right at the hotel reception entrance.
