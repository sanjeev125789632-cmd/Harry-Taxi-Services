Highway Reality

Chandigarh to Manali Highway Conditions & Waypoints

Key road segments, travel times, and landmark stops along the NH-21 highway corridor.

Expressway Highlights & Route Stops

  • Chandigarh to Kiratpur Sahib (70 km): Fast 4-lane Punjab highway traversing Rupnagar.
  • Kiratpur to Nerchowk (78 km): The modern Greenfield Expressway featuring 5 bypass tunnels that eliminate the old winding ghats around Swarghat and Bilaspur.
  • Nerchowk to Mandi & Pandoh (35 km): Transition to riverside drive following the Beas River, crossing Pandoh Dam.
  • Pandoh to Aut Tunnel (25 km): River-level mountain road leading into the famous 2.8 km Aut tunnel bypass.
  • Aut to Kullu & Manali (70 km): Wide, smooth 4-lane highway through the scenic apple valley, passing Kullu town into Manali (2,050 m).

Seasonal Travel Advice

Summer (April to June): Peak tourist season. Excellent road conditions. Expect evening traffic near Manali town; our drivers know alternate link roads via Naggar and Left Bank.

Monsoon (July to August): The Pandoh to Aut stretch is prone to heavy rain runoff. Our drivers monitor real-time HP Traffic Police updates and avoid high-risk night driving during active cloudburst alerts.

Winter (December to February): Frost and black ice can form between Kullu and Solang. Vehicles are equipped with winter tyre treads and fog lights.
